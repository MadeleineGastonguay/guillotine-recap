A cross-check: does the Simulations tab agree?

Everything above uses the Approximate tab, because it can be recomputed every five minutes of a week. The Simulations tab works differently. It plays out 5,000 whole weeks, draws each player's score from real, skewed outcomes, and re-picks every team's best lineup in each one, so a bench player who booms can step in. That is too slow to run at every step, so the two tabs are compared at seven moments per week: before the first game, five evenly spaced moments, and the kickoff of the week's last games.

How this was made

Each week is rebuilt from nflverse play-by-play, whose time stamps give every game its real clock, together with Sleeper's archived pregame projections and the league's scoring rules. At every five minutes while games were on, the tracker's own math is run on the state of the league at that moment. The model shown is the Approximate tab. A few players each week score differently in the play-by-play than in Sleeper's feed. The live tracker uses Sleeper's numbers directly, so this only touches the replays.
